H & S HOME SOLUTIONS — WEBSITE STARTER

FILES
- index.html: responsive storefront homepage, categories, product catalog, cart and order form
- script.js: sample products, cart logic and WhatsApp checkout
- design-preview.png: visual design mockup

QUICK START
1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the website on your computer.
2. Open script.js in Notepad or another text editor.
3. Replace 923XXXXXXXXX at the top with your actual WhatsApp number, in international format without +, spaces or dashes. Example format: 923001234567.
4. Edit sample product names and prices in the products array to match your actual stock.
5. Upload index.html and script.js to a static host to publish. Keep both files in the same folder.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a front-end starter website. The example products/prices are placeholders and should be checked/changed before selling.
- WhatsApp ordering works after setting the real business number.
- Online payment is not connected to a payment gateway. For real online payments, you will need a payment provider account and its approved integration. The checkout currently asks the customer to confirm payment details on WhatsApp.
- There is no secure admin dashboard, database, inventory system, or automatic shipping integration in this starter version.
- A free hosting subdomain may be available; a custom domain usually costs extra. Publishing requires you to create/authorize your own hosting account.
